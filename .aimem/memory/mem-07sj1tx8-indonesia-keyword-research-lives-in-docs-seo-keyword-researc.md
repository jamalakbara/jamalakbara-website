Indonesia keyword, intent and competitor research (OpenSEO, 8 Nov 2026) is in docs/SEO_KEYWORD_RESEARCH_ID.md. Findings that change copy decisions: head term "jasa pembuatan website" (9,900/mo) is held by marketplaces and hosting firms, and its KD 0 is misleading; the Bandung SERP is agencies plus a local pack and is the real target ("jasa website bandung" 1,600); deliverable pages (company profile 880, landing page 390, toko online 260) have the lowest competition; Shopify service keywords have almost no volume in Indonesia; price-check queries (biaya/harga pembuatan website, 320 and 260) are a gap. Nobody in the results sells "one person designs and builds it". Re-run once Search Console has Bahasa queries. OpenSEO project id e3a7449e-3b86-4f3e-a18a-030088b076cb, location 2360, language id.

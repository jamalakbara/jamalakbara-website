Brand SERP check (13 Nov 2026): jamalakbara ranks #1, but the shared name Jamal Akbar Alam is weak and the site mixed Akbar, Chairil Akbar and the full name. Decision: identity statements (about, intros, service intros, image alt, schema, keywords) use Jamal Akbar Alam. Chairil Akbar and Akbar were removed from schema alternateNames and meta keywords. Kept on purpose: the contact greeting 'Hi Akbar, I am {name}' (how visitors address the owner) and the body of the intro post halo-gue-akbar, which explains the nickname. Rejected: keeping Akbar as a public alias, because Google could not tell the profiles were one person. Off-site profiles (LinkedIn, GitHub, Instagram, X, Facebook, Pinterest, Contra, Dribbble, Behance) must match.

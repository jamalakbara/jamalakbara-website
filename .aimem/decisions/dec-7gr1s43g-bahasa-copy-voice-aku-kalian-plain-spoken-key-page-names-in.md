Bahasa copy (service pages, journal posts, site overlay) uses aku/kalian, informal spellings gak, pake, udah, and keeps page names Home, Work, About, Services in English. Why: the owner rejected an earlier draft as too AI-generated. Plain spoken Bahasa wins over polished lines: no calques from English (for example "lantai" for price floor), no slogan-style rhythm, "-in" verb endings only where a person would say them. The owner reviews and edits pages after drafting, and their edits are the source of truth (they changed CTA labels and spellings to gak).

Note: .claude/rules/seo.md and dec-qssdnxaa describe /jurnal posts as gue/lo. The existing published Bahasa posts and the owner drafts use aku/kalian, so new Bahasa copy follows aku/kalian.

Interface strings: Services stays English ("SERVICES", "Services"). Nav labels are Home, Work, Jurnal, About, Kontak (owner can change).

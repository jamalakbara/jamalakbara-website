# Brand-awareness content research (EN + Bahasa)

Run 12 Nov 2026 with OpenSEO. Companion to `SEO_KEYWORD_RESEARCH_EN.md` and `_ID.md` (8 Nov), which cover buyer intent. This pass covers informational, top-of-funnel queries: topics a future client reads before they know who Jamal is. About 330 credits used. EN = US (2840), ID = Indonesia (2360). Volumes are monthly Google estimates.

## 1. What changed since 8 Nov

- **Search Console (9 Aug to 9 Nov):** 29 queries, 0 clicks. The five Bahasa service pages are indexed and already show impressions at position 55 to 100, for example `jasa pembuatan aplikasi mobile` 23 impressions, `company profile` 15, `jasa website bandung` 12. That is normal for new pages. Re-check in 8 to 12 weeks.
- **Name queries:** `jamal akbar` avg position 15, `akbar jamal` 22. Home ranks about 5, `/about` about 7.6 (the only click).
- **Brand SERP is weak.** `jamal akbar alam` (read from Indonesia) did not show jamalakbara.com in the top 9: results were other people (Akbar Alam, several Muhammad Jamal Akbar profiles, Jamaludin Akbar). The US read on 8 Nov had the site at #4. The name is shared, so Google has not settled on one entity. Structured data already lists `sameAs` (GitHub, LinkedIn, X, Facebook, Instagram), so the remaining fix is off-site: consistent name, byline and profiles. Content alone will not own the name.

## 2. Reading the data

Keyword tools drift on question seeds (`how to choose a web developer` returned mostly unrelated terms), so candidates were hand-picked and scored with exact-match metrics, then checked against live SERPs.

Realistic ceiling: winnable awareness topics sit at **roughly 10 to 1,500 searches a month**. The big informational terms (`apa itu website` 5,400, `ui vs ux` 6,600, `apa itu seo` 4,400) are held by hosting companies, universities and design agencies. For a solo site with no backlinks, awareness here is built on a few pages that show a point of view and get cited, not on head-term traffic.

## 7. Bahasa-first pass (12 Nov, later)

Owner decision: focus on the Indonesian market first. 55 more Bahasa candidates scored; 8 SERPs read (about 150 credits).

**Finding:** the business-owner questions are tiny in volume (most 10 to 70 a month). The big volume is in pure explainers (`apa itu domain` 4,400, `apa itu hosting` 3,600, `wordpress adalah` 1,600), and those SERPs belong to hosting companies. So Bahasa posts here earn trust and internal links to the service pages; they will not bring large traffic alone. More weight should go to service pages, case studies and the Google Bisnisku profile.

# Keyword, intent and competitor research: Indonesia (Bahasa)

Run 8 Nov 2026 with OpenSEO (DataForSEO). Market: Indonesia (location 2360), language `id`. Volumes are monthly Google estimates. KD is OpenSEO keyword difficulty.

**Read KD with care.** Many head terms show KD 0 (for example `jasa pembuatan website`, 9,900). The live results say otherwise: platforms and hosting companies hold the top spots. Trust the SERP table below over the KD number.

**Weak or dead for Indonesia:**
- Shopify: `jasa shopify` and `jasa shopify developer` have almost no volume. `shopify indonesia` (880) is navigational, people looking for Shopify itself. Keep Shopify as a platform you use inside the toko online page, not a headline.
- UI/UX as a service: `jasa desain ui ux` 40, `jasa ui ux designer` 10. Fold it into the website pages. Use the topic only for journal posts.
- Bandung + app/landing/toko online: 10–30 searches each. Do not build city-modified pages for these.

**SERP features seen:** AI Overview on almost every query, local pack on every Bandung query, People Also Ask on head terms.

### What competitors do that is worth copying
- One landing page per vertical or deliverable (tonjoo: manufaktur; badoy: company profile, toko online, Solo).
- A tutorial cluster that links into the service pages.
- Titles with a trust word and a price hook.

### Gap you can own
- Nobody in these results sells "one person, designs and builds it". Every competitor reads as a studio or a marketplace. Your draft title ("dikerjain langsung sama yang bikin") already points at this.
- Concrete prices and process. Competitors hide them or lead with the lowest number.
- Case studies as proof. Few competitor pages show named client work.

## 4. Copy guidance from this data
1. Write the Bahasa pages for the hire-by-deliverable and Bandung queries. Put the exact phrase in the title, the H1 and the first paragraph, once each, then write naturally.
2. Lead with who does the work and what the client gets, not "murah" or "terbaik". Both words are saturated.
3. Show a price range or a "mulai dari" figure on each service page. It answers the price-check intent and the SERP rarely does.
4. Keep Shopify, React and Next.js as proof points inside pages, not as page topics.
5. English pages: the English market for your service is not worth targeting from Indonesia. Keep English as brand and international credibility.
6. Do not spend copy effort on freelance-job or portfolio-inspiration queries.

## 5. Caveats
- Volumes come from Google Ads data and are bucketed. Treat small numbers (under 100) as directional.
- OpenSEO reads each SERP several times and merges them. Positions vary between reads; the Bandung result pages shifted order between calls.
- Competitor traffic figures are estimates.
- Search Console is connected but has no queries for jamalakbara.com yet. Re-run after the Bahasa pages are indexed.

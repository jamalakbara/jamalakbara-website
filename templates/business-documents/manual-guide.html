Getting started.

Everything you need before your first edit. It takes about five minutes.

  1. 01

    Open the admin

    Go to [client-domain.com/wp-admin] in Chrome, Safari or Edge. Bookmark it so you can find it again.

  2. 02

    Sign in

    Enter the email and password sent to you separately. Tick Remember me only on your own device.

  3. 03

    Set your own password

    Open [Profile → Account], choose a new password of at least 12 characters, then press [Update profile].

  4. 04

    Check your role

    Your role decides what you can change. If a menu from this guide is missing, your role may not include it. See the table below.

04

Everyday tasks.

The jobs you will do most. Copy a task block to add your own.

A / [Add a new product]

[Products → Add new] / ~5 min

  1. 01

    Open [Products] and press [Add new].

  2. 02

    Fill in the name, price and description. Prices use the default currency, [IDR].

  3. 03

    Add photos under [Product gallery]. Use square images, at least 1200 × 1200 px.

  4. 04

    Pick a category, then press [Publish]. Check it on the live site.

B / [Edit a page's text]

[Pages → All pages] / ~2 min

  1. 01

    Find the page in the list and press [Edit].

  2. 02

    Click into any text block and type. Keep headings short so they fit on mobile.

  3. 03

    Press [Preview], then [Update] when it looks right.

C / [Replace an image]

[Media → Library] / ~2 min

  1. 01

    Upload the new image and fill in its [Alt text]: one plain sentence describing it.

  2. 02

    Open the page or product that uses the old image and choose the new one. Save.

05

Good practice.

Do

  • Compress photos before upload. Aim for under 500 KB each.
  • Preview every change before you publish it.
  • Write alt text for every image. It helps search and screen readers.
  • Keep page titles under 60 characters.

Avoid

  • Installing or updating plugins without checking with me first.
  • Changing a page's URL. Old links and Google results will break.
  • Pasting text straight from Word. Use paste as plain text.
  • Deleting a page instead of moving it to draft.
06

Troubleshooting.

If this happensTry this

I can't sign in

Use [Lost your password?] on the sign-in page. After five failed tries, wait 15 minutes.

My change isn't showing

Check that you pressed [Update], not only [Preview]. Then refresh with Ctrl + Shift + R (⌘ + Shift + R on Mac).

An image looks blurry

Upload a larger file, at least twice the size it appears on screen.

The layout looks broken

Undo your last change with [Revisions], then email me a screenshot and the page address.
